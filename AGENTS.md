# Repository instructions

Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) before changing this repository. Its architecture,
content rules, **Code quality** section, and applicable validation gates apply
to all coding agents and human contributors.

The **Task agents** workflow applies when assigned a task-board card in a named
worktree. A review requested directly by the maintainer may use its requested
branch and scope. Do not create issues, send messages, merge, push, or publish
unless the maintainer has authorized that action.

Keep review reports in `docs/`, with evidence, reproduction steps, severity,
validation results, and explicit limits. Do not change generated Quran or matn
data by hand. Preserve unrelated work and stop only processes started for your
own task, identified by PID.
